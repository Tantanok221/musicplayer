# MusicBee UI research and Sonora's implementation

The reference is MusicBee's library workspace, especially **Album and Tracks**, rather than a streaming-service home screen. Research was checked against the publisher's Microsoft Store screenshot and MusicBee's own community manual/screenshots. The manual pages describe older versions, so screenshots are used to corroborate their panel structure.

## Interaction contract

1. Top workspace tabs switch Music Explorer, Queue, Playlists, and Now Playing. Music Explorer is the main library page and retains its view and search/filter state. Queue contains the editable playing list; Now Playing highlights the current album and track.
2. The left panel contains navigation, playlists, and a thumbnail browser with a persisted Artist / Album Artist / Year / Genre dropdown. Track artists include individual guest credits; album artists retain discography navigation. Year and Genre open matching Explorer filters. Choices cover the library, years sort newest first, and counts deduplicate albums. Clicking an artist opens their discography; Command-click retains multi-filter selection. Artist links elsewhere open the same page. Albums, EPs, and Singles are separate sections, with Other releases for missing/other primary types. Membership uses album artists and collaborations, independent of library filters. Back and Forward buttons beside the window controls (also View menu, Command-[ and Command-]) move through visited workspaces, artist pages, and Explorer filters.
3. Optional Genre → Album Artist → Album browser columns filter left to right. Changing an upstream selection resets downstream selections; Command-click unions/toggles values inside a column. Counts and choices reflect upstream filters.
4. The central view switches between Album and Tracks, Album Covers, and Tracks. Album and Tracks shows successive album blocks with artwork beside compact track lists. Covers selects/expands an album in place. No separate oversized album landing page.
5. Selecting an album or track does not start playback or replace the queue. Playing an album replaces the queue with that full album. Double-clicking a song/Play Now replaces the queue with that song and all later songs on the same album in disc/track order, independent of filters. Existing queue rows jump within the current queue. Track and album context menus offer Play Next (after the current song), Play After This Album (after the current album's run in the queue), and Add to End of Queue (after the last queued album), each with a second line naming that song or album; the current track keeps playing.
6. The right sidebar simultaneously shows Queue and track information. Queue rows carry favorite and rating controls; narrow sidebars fold the stars into a rating menu. Hovering any star rating previews the rating a click would set, including clearing when the current rating is hovered. Details, Lyrics, and Artwork tabs have an explicit Selected/Playing source selector. Opening Lyrics follows the playing track; synchronized lyrics highlight and follow the active line, including after seeking. Clicking a timed line seeks to it. Plain lyrics remain readable without fabricated timing.
7. Navigation and information panels resize with dividers and can be hidden. The navigator can instead auto-hide: the library uses the full width, and touching the left window edge floats the navigator over it without reflowing albums until the pointer moves past its right edge. Their visibility, widths, browser visibility, view choices, and information-panel choices persist. Restore layout restores these settings.
8. The player is a compact, flat strip with previous, play/pause, and next controls, progress, volume, current-track feedback, and individual artist links. There is no Stop button. Status reports the current library/selection and queue separately.
9. Track lists stay simple: number/title, duration, favorite, and five-star rating; no spreadsheet table controls. This preserves the user's explicit preference.
10. Now Playing sits on a blurred copy of the album art under a theme-colored scrim, crossfading between albums (instant with Reduce Motion). The left column shows the cover, track title and artists, album details, six album tracks around the current one (expandable), and an Up Next list of the following queued tracks (eight shown, expandable; hidden when the queue is exactly this album); synchronized lyrics fill the right. A 48-band frequency spectrum (32 Hz–16 kHz, log-spaced, analyzed from actual output samples) runs along the bottom in the album's palette colors, with falling peak caps. Paused/buffering output settles to rest. Space remains text input while editing Search or other text fields.
11. Search double-clicks select text without zooming the window. Only the empty title-bar space owns window drag/double-click behavior.
12. Cover columns use the actual center-panel width and reflow when side panels resize or toggle. Expanded album rows stay within that panel; narrower layouts stack artwork above the tracks.
13. At compact window widths, workspace tabs stay on one horizontally scrollable row, with arrow buttons for mouse navigation. Selecting a workspace with a keyboard shortcut reveals its tab. Side panels share a width budget that reserves room for the library. Search remains visible. The player keeps one horizontal line in three groups: the current track with its favorite on the left; shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, and repeat beside elapsed time, seek, and total time in the center; rating and mute/volume on the right. Compact widths fold the five stars into a rating menu so seek keeps its space.
14. Expanded album panels put the linked artist on its own line beneath the album title, followed by separate, aligned Year, Genre, and Label rows. Missing fields are omitted, record-label names are individually linked, and the panel omits the track count. A heart beside the album title controls its favorite state, and five stars beneath the artwork control its rating without changing songs. Stars preview on hover, and clicking the current rating clears it. The header has no play button; cover double-click and the album context menu retain album playback. There is no repeated artist heading or caption below the artwork. Normal Album and Tracks artist groups retain their headings.
15. Lyric focus fades and eases between lines with a subtle accent rail and background. Auto-follow scrolls smoothly between line boundaries and leaves manual scrolling alone between transitions.
16. Artist credits split on ampersands, commas, and semicolons, including full-width variants. Individual names drive links, facets, counts, filters, and discography membership. Counts deduplicate shared albums; matching uses whole names rather than substrings. Combined artist IDs are not reused for each split credit.
17. Genre, year, and record-label metadata links open Music Explorer with a fresh filter. Genres are linked individually, preserving names such as R&B. Record labels use complete normalized names, including co-release labels, without splitting commas or ampersands. Metadata clicks do not select or play their enclosing album card. Active filter chips and Clear filters remove metadata filters; playback stays unchanged.

18. Playback requests original audio and opens output using the decoded source sample rate and channel count. It does not force 48 kHz stereo or fall back to MP3 transcoding. Unsupported formats show an error. Same-format tracks retain gapless boundaries; format changes may pause briefly while output reopens. Shared system output is not a bit-perfect guarantee.

19. Navidrome is the only scrobbling recipient. Listening instances survive decoder restarts and restoration; actual consumed samples determine eligibility. Unsent reports may wait offline, but attempted/uncertain reports are never automatically resent. Last.fm authorization remains in Navidrome.
20. Artist pages and artist sidebar modes share cached portraits. The page adds a plain-text biography (Read more appears only when it exceeds three lines) and profile link, library-derived genre chips, side-by-side Popular songs and Your most played columns (five rows, expandable to ten), and up to twelve similar artists. Popular songs is hidden when Navidrome returns none, and the columns stack in narrow panes. Unowned recommendations are muted, labeled, and open Last.fm; owned artists open Sonora. Release grouping and album-based playback remain unchanged.

## Server boundary

The user-entered Navidrome connection remains the source of music. A reusable login token is saved locally with owner-only file permissions; the password is not saved and Keychain is not accessed. No demonstration library, hardcoded credentials, fake waveforms, folder-management UI, or nonfunctional radio/device nodes are added. Local file tagging and MusicBee's broader plugin/device/CD features are beyond this UI adaptation.
